# 12 — Bounded critique and original-source fact-checking

## Dependencies

11. Full upstream tracing is completed in 13; do not falsely claim primary-origin verification here.

## Deliverables

- Separate-context, different-model section critique and original-writer repair.
- Structured evidence-backed issue ledger, severities, fixes/rejections, and unresolved checks.
- Original-source support/context/numeric verification and change-triggered rechecks.
- Budget enforcement: 3 section critique/repair and 2 fact-check/repair rounds.

## Acceptance criteria

- A critic cannot pass factual objections solely by assertion. Record the claim and source basis or a concrete missing check; reject false objections with reasons.
- Repairs do not erase qualifiers, invent new numbers, change species/populations, or add unverified citations without reopening checks.
- Confirmed major scientific defects/blockers prevent completion; minor style findings may remain. Faithful disagreement is not forced into consensus.
- Exhaustion produces an explicit blocked/human action, never an automatic pass. Model-created revisions do not reset budgets.
- The final validation pass is read-only. Tests count actual findings and repair calls, including early success/no-repair cases.
- A changed claim/source/version invalidates the appropriate earlier checks. A real citation to a secondary source is not labelled verified primary support.
- Persistent issue and approval state survives interrupted review calls and worker restart without duplicate accepted repairs.

## Handoff

Include call-count traces, false-critic and repair-regression fixtures, unresolved origin flags for 13, and blocking-state demonstrations. A different model is not described as independent human verification.
