# 11 — Section blueprints and evidence-grounded drafting

## Dependencies

10.

## Deliverables

- Persistent per-section planner/writer workspaces mapped to project/role/session IDs.
- Source suggestions and paragraph blueprints with sentence-level intents and evidence dependencies.
- Revision-specific blueprint approval, drafting packages, stable citation keys and manuscript assembly.
- Independent progression of approved sections under the one-active-project rule.

## Acceptance criteria

- No section drafts without its approved blueprint and global framework/outline revisions. Other unapproved sections do not unnecessarily block an approved section.
- Reconstruct context after worker restart from approved artifacts; no sole dependence on raw session history.
- A draft uses summaries plus relevant original evidence, with methods/context available for interpretation.
- Test compound claims, multi-source synthesis, unsupported generalization, direct versus secondary attribution, and novel author proposals.
- A citation is sourced from verified corpus metadata; no model-generated bibliography or fabricated study details are accepted.
- Shared argument changes flag affected blueprints/drafts. A section cannot silently invent its own definitions or widen scope.
- Draft status remains provisional: source checks and origin tracing are not yet complete. Do not export it as scientifically approved final output.

## Handoff

Record context construction, actual role/model pinning, source package examples, citation representation, and fixture failures. Later reviewers must be able to inspect every draft revision and its evidence dependencies.
